About SkillBooks

SkillBooks is an online store for digital learning products and creative assets: eBooks that teach practical skills and wallpapers that make your screens look better.

Our mission

Make useful digital skills easier to learn. Our eBooks focus on topics such as coding, AI, freelancing and creative work, written to be read at your own pace.

Our vision

A store where learners in Pakistan and across the world can find clear, affordable digital products in one place.

Digital learning

Each eBook page explains what the book covers, its category and its price, so you can decide before you buy.

Creative products

Wallpapers are shown as preview images. The original files are delivered to you after purchase.

How buying works

When you press Buy Now you are taken to Gumroad, which processes the payment and delivers your files. We do not collect or store payment card details.

Customer support

Questions about a product or this website: see the Contact page. For payment, receipt or download problems, use the support link in your Gumroad receipt email.
